What we built that QuickMail doesn't have

None of this exists in QuickMail. It is the reason the app exists.

What we added Why it matters
Ask before acting Sending a reply or blocking someone both stop and show you exactly what will happen. Nothing goes out on its own, and every decision is recorded.
Permanent block list QuickMail forgets someone if the spreadsheet is imported again. Ours remembers the email address forever, so a person who asked to stop is never contacted twice.
Reply sorting Every reply is labelled positive, neutral or negative. 375 conversations become the 138 actually worth reading — the rest are out-of-office messages.
Suggested replies A ready-written answer for each conversation, signed by whoever owns that mailbox. You edit it or replace it before it goes.
Campaign from a brief Describe the campaign in a sentence and the app writes the emails, the follow-ups and the schedule, then builds it in QuickMail.
Campaign start and end dates QuickMail has no such setting. We add it by switching the emails on and off at the right time.
Checks before sending Catches a broken sender, a missing name field, or a blocked address — each check added after a campaign quietly sent nothing.
Search inside replies Search the words in the message, not just names. "The one who asked about pricing" is findable.
